AdPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

AdPilot ("we", "our") is a Shopify app that helps merchants manage social media posting and advertising campaigns via Meta (Facebook/Instagram) APIs.

Data we collect

  • Shop domain and access tokens: Required for Shopify OAuth authentication. Stored securely.
  • Connected social accounts: Facebook Page IDs, Instagram account IDs, and Meta ad account IDs you connect, along with their OAuth access tokens. Used only to publish posts and manage ads on your behalf.
  • Scheduled posts and campaigns: Post content, media URLs, campaign settings, and schedules you create in the app.
  • Wallet transactions: Topup amounts and service fee deductions for billing records.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your customers' personal data (names, emails, addresses).
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not access your store's customer or order data.

Meta data

When you connect Facebook/Instagram accounts, we interact with Meta's APIs on your behalf per Meta's Platform Terms. Your Meta data is used only to perform the actions you request (posting, ad creation).

Data retention

When you uninstall AdPilot, all your shop data is deleted within 48 hours. You may also disconnect individual social/ad accounts at any time from the Accounts page, which deletes their stored tokens immediately.
