PixelPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

PixelPro ("we", "our") is a Shopify app that installs Meta, TikTok, Google, and Snapchat pixels on your storefront and fires standard ecommerce events.

Data we collect

  • Shop domain and access tokens: Required for OAuth authentication and API access. Stored securely.
  • Pixel settings: Your configured pixel IDs and tracking preferences, stored in your shop's metafields.
  • Event diagnostics: Anonymous event names (e.g. PageView, AddToCart) fired on your storefront, kept in memory for the diagnostics page. No shopper personal data is included.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect customer personal data (names, emails, addresses).
  • We do not track shoppers across stores.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Data retention

When you uninstall PixelPro, all your shop data is deleted within 48 hours via Shopify's mandatory webhooks.
