AdPilot Terms of Service

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Service

AdPilot is a social media management tool that lets you schedule posts and create advertising campaigns on Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram) from your Shopify admin. The app is free to install with no monthly subscription fees.

2. Pay-per-use pricing

AdPilot operates on a prepaid wallet system. You top up your wallet, and service fees are deducted per action:

  • Social post publish: $0.50 per post
  • Ad publish: Awareness $2, Traffic $2.50, Engagement $2.50, Sales $3 per ad
  • Extra creative: +$1 each; Extra ad set: +$1.50 each
  • Audience creation: $1 each ($1.50 for lookalike)
  • Full funnel (3 campaigns + 3 audiences): $10.50

Topup packs: $5, $10, $30, $90 (+$10 bonus), $240 (+$60 bonus).

3. No refunds

Service fees are deducted immediately when you publish a post, create an ad, or create an audience. All fees are non-refundable. Please review the fee breakdown shown before confirming any publish action.

4. Ad spend

AdPilot's fees cover our service only. Your Meta ad spend (daily/lifetime budgets) is billed separately and directly by Meta to your ad account's payment method. We do not handle your ad spend.

5. Meta compliance

You are responsible for ensuring your ads comply with Meta's Advertising Policies and all applicable laws. We may refuse to publish content that violates platform policies.

6. Account connections

You must have the necessary permissions on the Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, and ad accounts you connect. You may disconnect accounts at any time; disconnecting deletes stored access tokens immediately.

7. Limitation of liability

AdPilot is provided "as is". We are not liable for ad performance, account restrictions imposed by Meta, or any indirect damages. Our total liability is limited to the fees you paid in the 3 months preceding a claim.
